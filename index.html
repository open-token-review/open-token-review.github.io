Why this archive exists

An early investor's story

I joined the Remittix presale early and invested a four-figure amount in USD. I intentionally do not publish my wallet, exact purchase history, name or other identifying details here. My investor dashboard still works; my registered receiving wallet and RTX balance remain visible.

What changed was my confidence. Over a long period I watched launch expectations move, fundraising continue and new promotions appear while I still could not claim freely tradable presale RTX. I began asking harder questions. According to my personal experience, after posting criticism and warning other participants to evaluate the risks, I lost access to official/community spaces on Telegram and X. That is my account of what happened to me, not an independently verified finding about Remittix's moderation policy.

I created this site because a Telegram message can disappear and an X account can be blocked. A public archive can preserve the history: what was promised, when it was promised, what happened next, and what remains unresolved.

Privacy: exact wallet addresses, transaction hashes and identifying information are deliberately withheld.

The timeline matters more than the marketing

Earlier official FAQ

$18M soft cap → launch-date announcement

An official Remittix GitBook FAQ stated a $18M soft cap and said a launch date would be announced after it was achieved, with roughly three months anticipated before launch.

29 Dec 2025 / early 2026

9 February 2026 platform launch announced

Project communications promoted 9 February as a platform-launch date. A platform/wallet rollout is not the same event as TGE, distribution and a liquid market for presale RTX.

Later 2026

The key reveal moves to $32M

Later communications tied the reveal of a confirmed RTX exchange-launch date to reaching $32M, a materially different trigger from the earlier FAQ.

21 Sep 2026

Presale remains unresolved for holders

The presale remains active. This investigation has not independently verified completed distribution of presale RTX into a freely tradable, liquid market.

Evidence map

What can be supported today

Real iOS product footprint

Apple lists Remittix Wallet by Remittix Labs Limited. This is meaningful evidence of a real external product footprint, but it does not by itself verify presale economics or PayFi settlement.

CertiK footprint

Remittix has a public CertiK profile and a reviewed token-contract/security footprint. CertiK evidence supports real technical work; it is not a certification of every business or fundraising claim.

Remittix Markets exists

Remittix Markets is a functioning interface. Its Terms describe it as a front end to Hyperliquid; Remittix itself does not provide the underlying matching and settlement engine.

Launch conditions changed

Historical official documentation referenced $18M as the trigger for announcing launch. Later messaging moved the key launch-date reveal to $32M.

PayFi remains the key product test

The wallet now advertises crypto-to-fiat bank payouts. This investigation has not independently reproduced a public end-to-end crypto → conversion → bank-account payment through disclosed rails.

Fundraising not independently reconciled

Large totals are reported by the project and syndicated media, but this investigation has not produced a complete public on-chain/off-chain reconciliation of the claimed presale raise.

Dashboard balance ≠ realized value

A presale dashboard and “worth at launch” estimate are not market value. The decisive test is canonical RTX delivered on-chain with real liquidity and an ability to sell.

Exchange proof must come from exchanges

Community posts, sponsored articles and price pages are not treated as listings. Strong evidence is an exchange-native announcement with a matching contract, pair and trading/deposit timing.

Regulatory/payment chain remains unclear

For the crypto-to-bank proposition, we still seek a clear public chain connecting the operating entity, applicable regulator/licence, KYC/payment providers and settlement rails.

Evidence threshold

What would materially change the assessment?

Strong positive evidence

  • Concrete TGE/distribution date
  • Presale RTX claimable on-chain
  • Exchange-native listing with matching contract
  • Real liquidity and withdrawals
  • Independent end-to-end PayFi evidence

Not enough by itself

  • “Soon” announcements
  • Bonuses and mystery boxes
  • Price predictions
  • Sponsored/syndicated PR
  • Dashboard launch valuations

Strong negative evidence

  • Another material launch-condition change
  • Failure after a concrete distribution deadline
  • Regulatory or law-enforcement action
  • Canonical-contract mismatch
  • Loss of holder access at scale
